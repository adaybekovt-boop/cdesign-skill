## Short description
Freelance creative developer portfolio. Shader and motion-first index; co-author of Ponpon Mania. The site is a workshop wall, not a case-study CMS.

## Why it is visually interesting
A developer portfolio where experiments, shaders and commissioned pieces share one wall. Navigation is the work surface.

## Signature decision
Workshop-wall portfolio: experiments and clients in one index, with shader studies allowed to be first-class entries.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Experiments listed with the same dignity as client work
- No fake case-study framework around a shader study
- Type is functional; the motion entry is the personality
- Links out to Ponpon as a living project, not a buried 'also'
- Avoids the dark torus-knot developer cliché unless the study needs it
- Short labels over long case narratives on the index
- Personal site stays smaller than the side projects it points to

## Reuse as a principle
Let studies and clients share an index. Do not force every experiment into a 6-section case template.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy Patrick's projects, shaders, or lettering.

## Brand-bound elements
Patrick Heng name, project list, Ponpon credit.
