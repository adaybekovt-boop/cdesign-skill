## Short description
Riga studio behind FOLLOW.ART. Studio site mixes sharp product thinking with experimental type and object moments.

## Why it is visually interesting
A studio index that can jump from startup-clear structure into a sculptural moment without staying in one gear.

## Signature decision
Gear-shift studio: clear information architecture that is allowed to break into a poster or object beat mid-page.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- IA is readable like a product studio
- Then a beat goes poster/object
- Work pages can be louder than home
- Type sizes shift aggressively between gears
- Not permanently experimental, not permanently corporate
- Color can spike per case
- Opposite of a single-vibe template

## Reuse as a principle
Author two gears (clear IA / poster break) and switch once per page. Do not blend them into mush.
