# Neal.fun

- **URL:** https://neal.fun/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `neal-fun`

## Short description
Neal Agarwal's index of single-purpose browser experiments (Password Game, Infinite Craft, Stimulation Clicker…). Each toy is a site; the home is a shelf.

## Why it is visually interesting
Interaction design as a series of complete jokes. No studio manifesto. The index is handwritten and uneven on purpose.

## Signature decision
Shelf of finished toys: each experiment is a closed mechanic with its own URL, gathered by a human-looking index.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Home is a shelf, not a product
- Each toy has a complete rule set
- Visual style is allowed to change per toy
- No unified design system across experiments
- Humor is the UX writing
- Mobile toys are designed as toys, not shrunk desktops
- Opposite of a single cinematic universe

## Reuse as a principle
Ship a family of small complete mechanics instead of one endless page. Let the index look handmade. Do not force a shared visual system across jokes.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy specific Neal games, names, or illustrations.

## Brand-bound elements
neal.fun name and individual game IP.
