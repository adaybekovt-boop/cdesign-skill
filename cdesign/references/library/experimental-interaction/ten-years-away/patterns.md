## Hero composition
Principle: an intro beat can be a door into a long-form mechanic rather than a claim + CTA.

## Section structure
Principle: chapters = years = camera rooms. Uneven chapter length is more honest than equal sections.

## Navigation
Principle: combine a persistent chapter index with continuous reading. Either one alone is weaker.

## CTA placement
Principle: almost none. Anniversary pieces can end on a quiet credit, not a 'Work with us' slab mid-comic.

## Storytelling
Principle: bind three systems to one signal (here: scroll velocity → camera, shader smear, audio rate). One signal, many consequences.

## Cards
Principle: panels are not cards. They have gutters, reading order, and a camera.

## Grids
Principle: comic tier / gutter logic instead of marketing columns.

## Image treatment
Principle: one illustrator, ten visual dialects. Variation is year-character, not random style soup.

## Transitions
Principle: scatter-then-align for chapter changes. Avoid crossfades that flatten the gutter.

## Footer
Principle: credits and the 'one year later' note are part of the story, not a sitemap.

## Responsive transformation
Principle: lose the camera before you lose the reading order. Vertical swipe through panels keeps the comic; a squashed horizontal canvas does not.
