# Inkwell

- **URL:** https://inkwell.tech/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `inkwell`

## Short description
Stealth AI company site by SavoirFaire. A scroll-driven narrative that reveals posture without revealing product internals. Awwwards SOTD May 2025. Vue + OGL + GSAP + Rapier.

## Why it is visually interesting
A company with no public product ships a story-shaped site: process steps of architecture/fashion become the scroll spine, while a 3D wheel and headline micro-interactions carry character.

## Signature decision
Stealth narrative: name the domains (cybersecurity, spatial planning, fashion design) and the process steps, withhold the dashboard.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- No product UI screenshot in the first several viewports
- Process steps (Pre-Design → Permitting) are the section rhythm
- Two-color paper field (#F6F8FA / #1C272D) instead of AI-purple
- 3D wheel / transition objects used as chapter marks, not as a product hero
- Headline letters carry micro-interaction instead of a gradient sweep
- Footer and contact are interactive scenes, not a four-column sitemap
- Mobile is separately paced, not a stacked desktop

## Reuse as a principle
For a company that cannot show the product, scroll through the customer's process. Use one sculptural object as a chapter mark. Keep the palette to paper and ink.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy Inkwell copy claims, the wheel model, or SavoirFaire motion. Do not invent fake AI capabilities to imitate stealth.

## Brand-bound elements
Inkwell name, process-domain list, specific founder names on the page.
