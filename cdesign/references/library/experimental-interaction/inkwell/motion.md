## Entrance
Loader constructs the field. Then the definition.

## Scroll-linked behavior
Steps light or dock as they pass. Wheel rotates or dissolves on chapter boundaries.

## Section transitions
Object-led, not crossfade.

## Micro-interactions
Headline letter jitter / weight shift on hover. Small.

## Hover
Type and footer only.

## Cursor behavior
Default or a small ink dot. Not a flashlight unless the brief asks.

## Pinned sections
Pin the wheel during a chapter if it is acting as a mark. Unpin between.

## Parallax
Minimal. Paper should feel still.

## Shader / WebGL
OGL for the object. Physics optional. Do not fill the paper with particles.

## Timing character
Editorial, 400–900ms chapter moves. Micro 120–220ms on letters.

## Reduced-motion
Static paper, static wheel render, no letter jitter.
