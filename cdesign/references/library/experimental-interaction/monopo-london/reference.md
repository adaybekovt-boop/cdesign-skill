# Monopo London

- **URL:** https://www.monopo.london/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `monopo-london`

## Short description
London arm of Monopo. Frequently referenced for mouse-reactive scenes: the pointer is a light, a lens, or a character in the frame rather than a 40px circle.

## Why it is visually interesting
Cursor is content. Moving the pointer changes what the scene is willing to show.

## Signature decision
Pointer-as-lens: the mouse reveals, warps or lights a scene instead of dragging a branded follower.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Cursor action changes the image (reveal, light, warp), not just a custom PNG pointer
- Home can be a single reactive field
- Work is reached after you have played
- Motion is local to the pointer neighborhood
- No giant cursor halo
- Scene stays readable without the pointer — play is extra
- Opposite of magnetic buttons on a static photo

## Reuse as a principle
Use the pointer as a lens or light over one scene. Keep a readable rest state for touch and reduced-motion.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy Monopo scenes, clients, or their exact shader.
