## Hero composition
Principle: the first viewport can BE the collection, not a pitch for the collection. A numbered live index with one display sentence is enough.

## Section structure
Principle: two viewing modes for the same dataset (structured finding-aid vs horizontal stream) beat multiple unique section templates.

## Navigation
Principle: chrome can be limited to mode, time, and contact. The content stream is the menu.

## CTA placement
Principle: Contact is a persistent chrome item, not a mid-page conversion block. No 'See our work' because you are already in the work.

## Storytelling
Principle: process fragments narrate the studio better than polished case studies when the brief is research, R&D, or education.

## Cards
Principle: reject cards. Use index rows (number / title / year) that can expand into full-bleed fragments.

## Grids
Principle: a catalog column that is allowed to break into a free horizontal belt.

## Image treatment
Principle: show unused and unpublished frames as first-class assets. Crop incompleteness is a feature.

## Transitions
Principle: mode-change transitions (grid → stream) instead of route fades.

## Footer
Principle: the about layer is an entered space, not a sitemap footer.

## Responsive transformation
Principle: some spatial experiments should refuse small screens rather than become a list. If you must ship mobile, invent a different motif — do not squash the stream.
