## Entrance
Archive is already present; type and clock settle. No 2-second branded preloader sequence.

## Scroll-linked behavior
Movement through the numbered stream is the primary animation. Switching viewing mode reflows the same nodes.

## Section transitions
Not multi-page. Mode change is the section transition.

## Micro-interactions
Row hover previews a fragment. Theme toggle is instantaneous re-tint, not a 800ms theatrical wipe.

## Hover
Low amplitude. The stream motion is louder than hover.

## Cursor behavior
Default or slightly reduced pointer. No large follower blob.

## Pinned sections
The chrome (theme, clock, contact) stays pinned. The archive moves.

## Parallax
Mild depth between index type and fragment images only. Not multi-layer landscape parallax.

## Shader / WebGL
Used, if at all, to spatialize the archive — not as a background torus.

## Timing character
Slow catalog pace. Titles must remain readable while the stream is moving.

## Reduced-motion
Honor prefers-reduced-motion by freezing the stream in structured-index mode and dropping the mode-change animation.
