## Hero composition
Principle: title card of the story is the hero. Do not wrap a narrative product in a SaaS frame.

## Section structure
Principle: episodes then rooms (legend / team / support). Keep the count of rooms tiny.

## Navigation
Principle: a four-item textual index plus language pair. Reading is scroll.

## CTA placement
Principle: asks for support after affection exists, and in the project's voice.

## Storytelling
Principle: give a few panels agency (they react). Most panels can be still. Selective life beats constant motion.

## Cards
Principle: panels, not cards.

## Grids
Principle: gutters and reading order, not a 3×N icon grid.

## Image treatment
Principle: one illustrator's hand across the whole domain.

## Transitions
Principle: panel-to-panel acting. Route changes can be instant cuts.

## Footer
Principle: legal and support can exist; they should not look like a product footer.

## Responsive transformation
Principle: design the phone reading first for comics. Hover gags need tap doubles.
