# Ponpon Mania

- **URL:** https://ponpon-mania.com/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `ponpon-mania`

## Short description
Interactive animated webcomic by Justine Soulié and Patrick Heng. A megalomaniac sheep wants to become a DJ. Awwwards SOTM Oct 2025, Developer Award, SOTY nominee.

## Why it is visually interesting
Comic panels are alive: illustration, GSAP, and WebGL share the reading surface. Interaction is diegetic (you poke the world of the sheep), not a portfolio hover.

## Signature decision
A webcomic where each panel can contain a small interactive gag, with bilingual reading and a support path that stays in-world.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Reading, not pitching: the site is the comic
- Panel-level interactions (gags, loops, hover triggers) instead of page-level spectacle
- EN | FR as a first-class reading control, not a footer afterthought
- Characters (Ponpon, Jean-Loup, Simon) are the interface metaphors
- Support / merch ask is framed in-character, not as a Stripe pricing table
- Mobile is a designed reading mode for a comic, not a collapsed desktop WebGL scene
- Art direction is flat, bright, character-led — opposite of dark agency chrome

## Reuse as a principle
Build long-form narrative as a sequence of panels where a few panels are toys. Keep interaction inside the fiction. Language toggle belongs next to the reading surface.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy Ponpon, the sheep design, the flat color world, character names, or the specific gags. Do not clone the 'megalomaniac sheep DJ' premise.

## Brand-bound elements
Ponpon character IP, Justine Soulié drawings, Patrick Heng motion authoring, Tipeee/Ko-fi support framing.
