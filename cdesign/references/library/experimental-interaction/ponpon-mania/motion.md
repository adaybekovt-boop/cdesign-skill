## Entrance
Title card, then the first panel. Keep it short.

## Scroll-linked behavior
New panels enter as you read. Not a pinned 3D scene for the whole episode unless a gag needs it.

## Section transitions
Cuts between rooms. Inside an episode, motion is local to the panel.

## Micro-interactions
Gags: blink, bounce, tiny loops. 200–400ms. Funny > smooth.

## Hover
Only where a gag lives. Idle panels stay still.

## Cursor behavior
Default or a tiny in-world pointer. Never a 40px blob over a comic.

## Pinned sections
Rare. Pin only if a gag requires a stage.

## Parallax
Avoid on reading surfaces; it fights lettering.

## Shader / WebGL
Used as a special effect inside a panel, not as the page chrome.

## Timing character
Acting timing (holds, takes, snap-backs). Not luxury ease-out everywhere.

## Reduced-motion
Still panels, no loops, keep lettering readable. Story must survive.
