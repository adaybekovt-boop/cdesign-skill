## Short description
Creative developer known for tightly authored interaction and type. Portfolio as a precise instrument rather than a playground dump.

## Why it is visually interesting
Restraint as experiment: few effects, each one tuned. Opposite of the everything-moves developer site.

## Signature decision
One-effect-per-surface discipline: a page is allowed a single authored interaction and nothing competing.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Spectacle budget visibly enforced
- Type is the other actor
- No particle wallpaper
- Case entries isolated
- Timing feels authored, not default ease
- Color count low
- Opposite of 'I know WebGL' hero flex

## Reuse as a principle
Cap each viewport at one interaction idea. Spend the time on timing.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy Aristide's projects or type choices.
