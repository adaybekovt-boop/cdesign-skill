# Immersive Garden

- **URL:** https://immersive-g.com/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `immersive-garden`

## Short description
French studio (Awwwards Agency of the Year 2025). Studio site is a controlled index; the loud WebGL lives in commissions (Cartier etc.), not as wallpaper on the studio home.

## Why it is visually interesting
A useful anti-pattern breaker: the studio that can do spectacle often shows a quieter door. Experiment here is editorial restraint at the index.

## Signature decision
Quiet-door studio: the index is calm so each commission can be a different universe.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Home does not run the loudest possible WebGL
- Each work is a universe with its own rules
- Index type is calm and French-editorial
- Awards are not a trophy grid
- Contact is ordinary
- Opposite of studios that put a shader on the wordmark
- Teaches generated sites to isolate spectacle per route

## Reuse as a principle
Keep the index quieter than the work. Isolate spectacle inside entries. Do not preview every shader on home.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy Immersive Garden commissions (those belong to product/3D if used at all).

## Brand-bound elements
Immersive Garden name and commission list.
