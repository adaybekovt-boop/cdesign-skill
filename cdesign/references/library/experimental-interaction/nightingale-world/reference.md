# Nightingale

- **URL:** https://www.nightingale.world/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `nightingale-world`

## Short description
Immersive narrative / world site. Known for atmosphere-first navigation where sound, dusk palette and slow camera replace a conventional landing.

## Why it is visually interesting
A world you enter. Menu items are places. Timing is nocturnal and slow on purpose.

## Signature decision
Nocturnal world-entry: dusk palette, sound-first chrome, places instead of pages.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Sound is part of the first decision
- Places not pages
- Slow camera instead of punchy agency snaps
- Palette stays inside dusk/night
- Type is sparse and placed like signage
- Opposite of a bright product hunt page
- Mobile gets a quieter night poster, not a squeezed world

## Reuse as a principle
When the brief is a world, name places and put sound in chrome. Keep a silent poster fallback.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy Nightingale assets, story, or exact dusk grades.

## Brand-bound elements
Nightingale world IP.
