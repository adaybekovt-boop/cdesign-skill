# Justine Soulié

- **URL:** https://justinesoulie.fr/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `justine-soulie`

## Short description
Art director / illustrator site. Color, characters and motion studies; co-author of Ponpon Mania. A picture-led index rather than a developer HUD.

## Why it is visually interesting
Illustration portfolios often collapse into Dribbble grids. This one treats characters and color tests as navigable objects.

## Signature decision
Character-led index: the people/creatures in the drawings are the wayfinding, not a hamburger.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Characters as wayfinding
- Color tests as pages
- No developer chrome
- Ponpon lives as a world link
- Soft analog motion (blink, bob) instead of shaders
- Light grounds
- Opposite of a black WebGL CV

## Reuse as a principle
Let mascots or recurring figures be the menu. Keep analog motion small.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy Justine's characters or Ponpon.

## Brand-bound elements
Justine Soulié drawings and characters.
