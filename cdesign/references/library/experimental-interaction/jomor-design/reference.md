# Jomor Design

- **URL:** https://jomor.design/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `jomor-design`

## Short description
Portfolio known on Awwwards for scrolling portfolio navigation — works as a vertical index where each project is a full-bleed chapter you pass through rather than click into from a grid.

## Why it is visually interesting
Navigation and portfolio are the same object. You do not pick a thumbnail; you travel the reel.

## Signature decision
Portfolio-as-reel: project identity (name, role, still) occupies a full viewport and yields to the next on scroll.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- No thumbnail grid gateway
- Project name typography is oversized and changes per chapter
- Scroll is the selector
- Detail pages are destinations after the reel, not the home
- Negative space around a single still beats a masonry dump
- Hover may preview motion but scroll remains king
- Opposite of a filtered CMS portfolio

## Reuse as a principle
Let the home be a reel of full-viewport chapters. Filters and thumbnails can live one level deeper.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy Jomor's projects, lettering, or exact reel pacing.

## Brand-bound elements
Jomor identity and client work.
