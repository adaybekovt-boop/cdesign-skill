# The Creative Website Manual™

- **URL:** https://www.thecreativewebsitemanual.com/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `the-creative-website-manual`

## Short description
Huy Nguyen + Ivor Jian. A visual/technical manual on how creative sites are made — grids, type, motion — as a scrollable artifact. CSSDA WOTD Nov 2025.

## Why it is visually interesting
A meta-site: the subject is craft, the form is a manual. Chaptered scroll with typographic exhibits instead of a blog layout.

## Signature decision
Manual-as-site: chapters on grid/type/motion taught by being performed.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Curriculum is the IA
- Examples are live, not screenshots of other sites only
- Typographic exhibits
- Trademarked title as a graphic object
- Not a course landing with prices in the hero
- Grid is both topic and structure
- Opposite of a listicle '10 tips'

## Reuse as a principle
When teaching craft, perform the craft in the chapter. Make the table of contents the nav.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy the Manual™ title lockup or their exhibits.

## Brand-bound elements
The Creative Website Manual™ naming and exhibits.
