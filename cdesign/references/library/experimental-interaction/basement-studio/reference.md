# Basement Studio

- **URL:** https://basement.studio/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `basement-studio`

## Short description
Buenos Aires studio site. Known for hard type, dark wit, and interaction that feels like a tool rather than a luxury film.

## Why it is visually interesting
Attitude is the system: compressed type, abrupt color, interactions that click like hardware. Opposite of soft glass agency templates.

## Signature decision
Hardware-click studio: abrupt type sizes, dry copy, interactions with mechanical timing rather than cinematic overshoot.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Copy is dry and specific, not 'we craft experiences'
- Type sizes jump, they do not scale politely
- Interaction timing is mechanical (press, snap) rather than springy
- Work is listed with the bluntness of a directory
- Color can be sour or industrial, not navy-and-gold
- Cursor/hover often feels like a tool highlight
- Rejects both luxury black and pastel startup

## Reuse as a principle
Give a studio or tool site a mechanical timing chart and blunt directory energy. Let type jump sizes instead of easing between nearby steps.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy Basement's voice, jokes, or project list. Do not fake Argentine slang.

## Brand-bound elements
Basement name, studio voice, client roster.
