# Paper Tiger

- **URL:** https://www.papertiger.com/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `paper-tiger`

## Short description
US studio site, Awwwards SOTD Jan 2026. Webflow-built, pink signal on white (#FFFFFF / #F19EC2), interaction used as attitude rather than WebGL spectacle.

## Why it is visually interesting
Proof that experimental interaction is not only WebGL. Timing, color signal and copy ('infuriate their competition') carry the site.

## Signature decision
Signal-color attitude site: one loud pink on white, interaction as punchline, no 3D required.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Two-color white/pink field instead of dark WebGL
- Copy is combative and specific
- Webflow as the production constraint, motion still authored
- No product 3D
- Interaction is hover and page-enter, not a film
- Studio positioning is a one-liner, not a process diagram
- Opposite of both navy luxury and shader-agency

## Reuse as a principle
Pick one signal color on paper and let interaction be punchy and short. Constraint (Webflow) can still yield awarded motion.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy the pink hex as a preset or the 'infuriate' line.

## Brand-bound elements
Paper Tiger name, pink signal, client list.
