# Resn

- **URL:** https://resn.co.nz/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `resn`

## Short description
Wellington studio. Long record of unusual navigation, WebGL toys and campaign microsites. The studio site is a cabinet of experiments rather than a sober list.

## Why it is visually interesting
A studio that treats navigation as a playground. Historical work includes scroll-type experiments and 'little helpers' — tools that exist to be played.

## Signature decision
Cabinet-of-experiments studio: entering work feels like opening a drawer that has its own physics.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Navigation can be a toy
- Type experiments are first-class
- Campaigns and self-initiated tools share the cabinet
- Color is allowed to be loud
- Does not flatten history into one current visual identity
- Play is not hidden behind a 'Labs' ghetto only
- Opposite of a beige consultancy

## Reuse as a principle
Allow nav to be a drawer with physics. Keep a boring list as a skip link for accessibility.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy Resn projects or the exact helper toys.

Resn name and campaign IP.
