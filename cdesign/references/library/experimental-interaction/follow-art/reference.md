# FOLLOW.ART

- **URL:** https://follow.art/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `follow-art`

## Short description
Vide Infra promotional site for an artist/curator network. Webby, Awwwards SOTD + Dev, CSSDA WOTD, FWA of the Day.

## Why it is visually interesting
A platform pitch told with rebellious display type, loud color fields and sculptural 3D that refuse marketplace-grey. The product (a shareable Card) is treated like a physical object in the layout.

## Signature decision
The Card-as-object: a shareable professional identity is staged as a thing you can almost hold, surrounded by shouting typography rather than SaaS screenshots.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Headline fragments stack as posters ('One Practice. One Card') instead of a 12-word value prop
- The Card is a physicalized object in the composition, not a UI screenshot in a laptop frame
- Color is loud and poster-like, opposite of art-world beige or SaaS grey
- How-it-works is a short numbered practice list, not a 6-icon grid
- No algorithm-feed metaphor — they reject it in copy and in layout
- 3D is accent sculpture, not a product configurator
- Awwwards-level motion on a real two-sided marketplace pitch

## Reuse as a principle
Physicalize the key token of a network (card, pass, badge) and let display type shout around it. Numbered practice beats icon features.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy FOLLOW.ART naming, Card UI, Vide Infra 3D sculptures, or the exact poster stacking.

## Brand-bound elements
FOLLOW.ART wordmark, Card product, Connectory name, Vide Infra sculptural language.
