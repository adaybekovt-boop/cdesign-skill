## Hero composition
Principle: a gate can be the first composition — hold, size check, sound recommendation.

## Section structure
Principle: chapters of a film, not sections of a landing.

## Navigation
Principle: HUD (chapter + progress) replaces a site menu for the duration.

## CTA placement
Principle: only after the last chapter.

## Storytelling
Principle: one verb (scroll) advances plot, camera, and world state together.

## Cards
Principle: none during the film.

## Grids
Principle: HUD corners, not content grids.

## Image treatment
Principle: projection mapping + post (CRT) to afford a city without a AAA budget.

## Transitions
Principle: story beats, not route wipes.

## Footer
Principle: end card with one outbound link.

## Responsive transformation
Principle: refuse small viewports for a desktop film. Do not ship a broken half-film.
