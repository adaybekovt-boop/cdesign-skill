# The Spark — The Digital Panda

- **URL:** https://spark.thedigitalpanda.com/
- **Category:** experimental-interaction
- **Slug:** `the-spark`

## Short description
Browser short film: a spark of creativity escapes into a city that wants it contained. Scroll drives the story. Built with cables.gl + Webflow. CSSDA WOTD Dec 2025.

## Why it is visually interesting
It refuses to start with a click. Press-and-hold is the ignition. Sound is on by default as a recommendation, and a too-small window is rejected with a written lockout.

## Signature decision
Hold-to-start gate + window-size lock + scroll-as-projector for a chaptered 3D short.

## Decisions that differ from a typical AI landing page
- Press & Hold to Start instead of a play button or auto-scroll
- Explicit minimum-hardware copy (laptop + headphones) before the film
- Window-too-small lockout with voice ('Oh snap!') instead of a squeezed WebGL view
- Chapter chrome (CHAPTER // 01) and a progress percent sit over the world
- Sound On/Off is first-class chrome, not a buried mute
- End card is a quiet ask ('let us help yours') after the story, not a hero CTA
- Projection-mapped city (game-dev trick) rather than a dense unique mesh per building

## Reuse as a principle
Gate heavy stories behind hold-to-start and a minimum viewport. Treat sound as part of art direction. Use chapter + progress as film chrome, not SaaS tabs.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy the panda character, neo-Tokyo city, cables.gl scene, or 'PROPERTY OF' stamping. Do not copy the uprising plot.
