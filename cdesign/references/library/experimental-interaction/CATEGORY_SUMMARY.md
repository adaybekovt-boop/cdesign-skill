## Design directions found

1. **Archive-as-space** — unpublished work as a navigable field with viewing modes (Obys Experiment Space).
2. **Instrument hero** — the product is a live tester, slider, or engine (Exat, Smooothy, Lenis).
3. **Camera-through-panels** — scroll drives a comic/film camera, not a document (Ten Years Away, Ponpon, The Spark).
4. **Hold / size / sound gates** — the experience refuses to start cheaply (The Spark, Obys desktop lock).
5. **Pointer-as-lens** — mouse changes the scene, not a 40px follower (Monopo, Ten Years Away halftone trail).
6. **Portfolio-as-reel** — one project per viewport (Jomor).
7. **Portfolio-as-world** — portals and rooms (Samsy) with a list fallback.
8. **Cabinet / drawer navigation** — nav is a toy plus a skip-link (Resn).
9. **Quiet index / loud work** — isolate spectacle per route (Immersive Garden, Hot Type, Studio375 house vs comic).
10. **Mechanical vs analog vs nocturnal timing** — three clocks that are not 'smooth luxury' (Basement, Analog, Nightingale).
11. **Stealth process itinerary** — tell the customer's steps, hide the dashboard (Inkwell).
12. **Physicalized token** — Card/pass as the object (FOLLOW.ART).
13. **Handmade toy shelf** — complete single-mechanic sites (Neal.fun).
14. **Density dial** — one-actor restraint (Aristide) vs live study wall (LHBVR).
15. **Signal-color punch** — experimental without WebGL (Paper Tiger).
16. **Manual-as-site** — teach craft by performing it (Creative Website Manual).
17. **Character wayfinding** — figures as menu (Justine Soulié).
18. **Shared-clock scroll** — interpolation exists to sync WebGL and DOM (Lenis manifesto).

## Patterns that repeat too often — do NOT make them defaults

- Dark full-viewport WebGL wallpaper behind a giant word.
- Custom cursor larger than 20px that does not change the scene.
- Smooth-scroll interpolation on pages that have no WebGL clock to sync.
- Magnetic buttons + particles + shader + cursor all in one viewport.
- Hiding the native scrollbar.
- Parallax on body text.
- Thumbnail masonry as the only portfolio model.
- Autoplaying sound with no control and no connection gate.
- Squeezing a desktop film or room onto a 390px screen.
- 'Labs' ghetto that hides experiments from the main index — or the opposite: shader on the studio wordmark.
- Purple-pink AI gradient (already banned in anti-slop; still appears on 'experimental AI' pitches).
- Identical hover-scale on every card.
- Centered hero with Play reel button as the only interaction idea.

## 10 best reusable ideas

1. Two viewing modes for one dataset (structured index ↔ horizontal stream).
2. Working instrument in the first viewport (tester, slider, engine).
3. Bind secondary systems to one signal (velocity → camera + smear + audio).
4. Gate heavy stories (hold, size, sound recommendation).
5. Pointer as lens/light/print-tool, with a rest state.
6. Reel home (one project per viewport) instead of masonry.
7. Quiet index, spectacle isolated per route or sibling URL.
8. Complete single-mechanic toys on their own URLs, handmade shelf on home.
9. Mechanical / analog / nocturnal timing charts as alternatives to luxury lerp.
10. Skip-link list fallback next to any spatial or toy navigation.

## 10 anti-patterns / overused solutions

1. Custom cursor as a large blurred circle that ignores content.
2. Smooth scroll as a vibe with no shared-clock reason.
3. WebGL room with no directory fallback.
4. Campaign takeover that permanently replaces the house site.
5. One shader identity painted across every project.
6. Particle field + magnetic CTA + pinned scrub in the same viewport.
7. Autoplaying film on mobile data.
8. Kinetic type that cannot rest for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
9. Thumbnail grid with identical 1.05 scale hovers called 'experimental'.
10. Copying a named studio's wordmark, characters, or comic as if they were primitives.
