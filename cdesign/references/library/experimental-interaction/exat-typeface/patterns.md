## Hero composition
Principle: put a working instrument (tester, configurator, playground) in the first viewport and let one sentence name the tension the instrument demonstrates.

## Section structure
Principle: alternate a tool surface with a cultural or historical passage. Specs arrive as matrices, not icon rows.

## Navigation
Principle: a type or tool site can drop global nav if scroll order is the curriculum.

## CTA placement
Principle: licensing / purchase can wait until after the instrument has been used. Do not crown the tester with twin buttons.

## Storytelling
Principle: origin story (movement, city, group) can be the spine of a product page when the product is cultural as well as technical.

## Cards
Principle: replace cards with a width×weight matrix or a name list that doubles as texture.

## Grids
Principle: specimen grids should expose the system (widths vs weights), not a bento of marketing tiles.

## Image treatment
Principle: letters are images. Avoid stock photography behind type.

## Transitions
Principle: mouse-driven glyph motion is the transition language. Page-to-page fades are secondary.

## Footer
Principle: language support and glyph count can live as a closing specimen, not a sitemap.

## Responsive transformation
Principle: keep the tester; collapse rails and matrices. Do not replace the instrument with a static pangram on mobile.
