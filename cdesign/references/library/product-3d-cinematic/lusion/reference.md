# Lusion

- **URL:** https://lusion.co/
- **Also:** https://labs.lusion.co/
- **Type:** Studio site as explorable 3D production world
- **Awards:** Awwwards SOTY 2023 (v3), multiple SOTD / FWA / CSSDA

## What it is
Bristol studio site where the homepage is not a marketing page with a 3D decoration. The page *is* a constructed physical space: objects, lights, and camera paths carry the studio's work. Projects appear as objects you encounter rather than cards in a grid.

## Why it is visually useful
Most AI landings put a torus or floating product over a dark gradient and call it 3D. Lusion treats 3D as set design: gravity, collision, scale contrast between UI type and world objects, and a camera that has a job (enter, orbit, descend) instead of idle spin.

## Signature decision
The WebGL world is the primary layout surface. HTML type is a thin overlay that never competes with the scene for spectacle. Scroll and cursor change the *camera and physics*, not just opacity of cards.

## 5 decisions that break the default AI landing
1. Hero is an inhabited volume, not a centered headline + CTA + screenshot.
2. Work items are spatial encounters (object, scale, lighting) instead of identical 3-up cards.
3. Motion has weight: objects settle, bounce, cast contact shadows; they do not float in a vacuum.
4. Type is large but secondary to the scene; it labels the world rather than filling it.
5. R&D (labs.lusion.co) is separated from the commercial face, so experiments do not leak into every section.
6. Lighting is motivated (practicals, HDRI, bloom used as atmosphere not as a filter on everything).

## Reuse as principle
Treat one 3D scene as the page architecture. Decide camera verbs first (enter / orbit / descend / dock). Place copy as captions on the scene, not as a second layout system fighting it.

## Do not copy directly
Do not copy their physics toys, mascot objects, specific project models, or the "scroll to explore" lab-style prompts. Do not rebuild Lusion's world with different labels.

## Brand-locked
Studio name, project reel, client list, labs URL, and the specific creature/object vocabulary they invented for themselves.
